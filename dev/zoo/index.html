The HRV Variable Zoo

A browsable "Pokédex" of every heart-rate-variability feature HeartRateLab computes: 53 registered measures across four domains. Each entry answers the same three questions:

  1. What is it? — definition, equation, aliases, and declared distribution family.
  2. What does normal look like? — the empirical distribution over the pooled nsrdb + nsr2db normal-sinus-rhythm cohorts (360-beat windows, n up to 56 472), overlaid with a fitted normative prior, plus a normal-range table.
  3. What does it cost, and who introduced it? — a measured wall-clock + allocation resource tier, curated use-cases, and the seminal citation.

Priors and normal-range statistics are descriptive references from a healthy cohort, not clinical thresholds. See the References page for the full bibliography. Resource tiers are measured on synthetic realistic RR at a 360-beat window (docs/zoo_gen/bench_resources.jl).

Resource tiers: ◍◌◌◌◌ very low · ◍◍◌◌◌ low · ◍◍◍◌◌ moderate · ◍◍◍◍◌ high · ◍◍◍◍◍ very high.
