Case A z-score reconciliation

JuliaCon 2026 poster · Heart Rate Oscillations during Meditation cohort · read-only forensic analysis, 2026-07-29

The discrepancy. The accepted abstract reports Case A (meditation) z-scores of Mean IBI +1.019, pNN50 +1.353, RMSSD +0.624, SDNN +0.776, drawn on the poster forest (juliacon2026_poster.tex lines 223–226 as 1.35 / 1.02 / 0.78 / 0.62). Recomputing from the shipped data (test/testdata/meditation/windowed_w360_s120_features.csv, 2686 windows, vs docs/normative_priors.csv, 56 472 pooled nsrdb+nsr2db windows): Mean IBI reproduces exactly, pNN50 reproduces exactly under one method, but RMSSD and SDNN do not reproduce under any aggregation × method (best current estimate +0.94 / +0.90). The accepted abstract also calls pNN50 "reduced" while +1.353 is an increase — an internal contradiction.

1. Method definitions tested

For feature value x and healthy-prior CDF F (params from docs/normative_priors.csv):

  • Quantile z-equiv (the method the poster + docs/specs/2026-07-06-…-poster-design.md claim): z = Φ⁻¹(F(x)).
  • Moment z: z = (x − μ_prior) / σ_prior. For a Normal prior the two are identical — so Mean IBI cannot distinguish them.

Priors used: mean = Normal(778.07, 143.71); pnn50 = Beta(0.4272, 10.3405); rmssd = Gamma(2.7000, 12.1746); sdnn = Gamma(4.0192, 13.1164). Feature aggregate = median (or mean) across the cohort's windows.

4. Root cause

  1. RMSSD/SDNN are stale/preliminary. Their abstract z's map to observed medians (~42–45 / ~70–73 ms) below what the shipped CSV yields (49.98 / 74.68 ms). They were computed from an earlier meditation feature extraction (different preprocessing or a smaller record subset) that is not committed to this repo. They are not recoverable from the shipped data under any aggregation × method.
  2. Latent method drift. The abstract's pNN50 (+1.353) reproduces exactly only under moment z on the all-windows median; the poster's declared quantile z-equiv gives +1.323. Mean IBI is identical under both, which masked the difference. So even the pNN50 number was produced by a method the poster no longer claims.
  3. Wording error. "reduced pNN50" contradicts z=+1.353 (an increase). pNN50 sits ~1.3σ above the healthy-population mean, coherent with the elevated Mean IBI (vagally-dominant meditation state).
