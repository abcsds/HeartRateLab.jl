# Studio B1 — ECG / QRS Waveform Marks

Lane: Direction B (HRV-first), specializing in **ECG / QRS complex and rhythm-strip**
marks. No Julia three-dot arrangement anywhere in this set. Julia palette is
carried through color only.

---

### b1ecg-01 — "QRS Line"
- **concept**: The honest P-QRS-T complex as a single confident stroke — no
  container, no ornament. The HRV identity leads entirely through the
  waveform's own silhouette.
- **motif**: ECG
- **colors**: Julia purple `#9558B2` only — a deliberately restrained,
  single-color mark.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: The single most literal, unmistakable "this is an ECG"
  read in the set; survives any size or background.
- **risk**: Single-color mark leans less on the Julia palette than others in
  the set; could be mistaken for a generic medical/health icon out of context.

### b1ecg-02 — "Capsule Monitor"
- **concept**: A two-tone pill/capsule tile (purple/green) with a bold white
  rhythm trace running through it, like a monitor-lead or medication capsule
  crossed with a heartbeat.
- **motif**: ECG (in a container)
- **colors**: purple `#9558B2` + green `#389826` halves, white trace.
- **register**: scientific / app-icon
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: The capsule silhouette is unusual enough to be
  memorable while the white-on-color trace reads instantly as "heart
  monitor."
- **risk**: The pill shape could read ambiguously as "medicine" rather than
  "HRV" without the trace doing most of the identification work.

### b1ecg-03 — "Pulse Tile"
- **concept**: A classic rounded-square app-icon tile in Julia purple with a
  bold white ECG trace knocked through the center — the most "install this
  app" flavored mark in the set.
- **motif**: ECG (in a container)
- **colors**: purple `#9558B2` fill, white trace.
- **register**: scientific / app-icon
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Extremely legible at favicon size; the tile form factor
  is exactly what package/app icons are judged against.
- **risk**: Single-color tile is the least "Julia-coded" of the set on its
  own (no multi-color palette use); relies on the family context.

### b1ecg-04 — "Tri-Lead Stack"
- **concept**: Three stacked rhythm-strip leads, one in each core Julia
  color, gesturing at multi-lead / multi-channel recording — the ecosystem
  nod lives in the *three colors*, not three dots.
- **motif**: ECG, multi-lead
- **colors**: purple `#9558B2`, green `#389826`, red `#CB3C33` — the three
  core Julia hues, deliberately in place of the three-dot glyph.
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Reads as genuine biomedical-signal vocabulary (a real
  3-lead strip) while still delivering the "three Julia colors" cue the
  ecosystem expects.
- **risk**: At extreme miniaturization the three thin lanes have the least
  margin of the set before merging into visual noise.

### b1ecg-05 — "R-Peak Bolt"
- **concept**: The QRS complex reduced to its single most essential gesture —
  a bold Q-R-S zigzag with no P/T waves at all, treated as a standalone bolt
  icon.
- **motif**: ECG, R-peak
- **colors**: linear gradient purple `#9558B2` → blue `#4063D8` (the
  brief's "use blue sparingly" accent gets a starring turn here).
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: The most abstracted, most favicon-proof mark in the
  set — thick, few points, unmistakable spike silhouette.
- **risk**: Without the P/T context some viewers may read it as a generic
  "lightning bolt / power" icon rather than specifically cardiac.

### b1ecg-06 — "Rhythm Ring"
- **concept**: A continuous circular monitoring loop built from four
  quarter-arcs, one per Julia color, broken at the top by a single red
  R-peak spike breaking through the ring — rhythm as an unbroken, looping
  process.
- **motif**: ECG, pulse/loop
- **colors**: all four Julia colors — purple, green, red (spike accent),
  blue — one per quadrant.
- **register**: scientific / geometric
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Elegant way to use all four palette colors at once
  without a three-dot cliché, and the segmented ring doubles as a "continuous
  monitoring" metaphor.
- **risk**: At very small sizes the ring reads more as a generic
  segmented-loader icon than specifically "heartbeat" — the spike is the
  only cardiac cue and it's the smallest element.

### b1ecg-07 — "HRL Pulseline"
- **concept**: Bold "HRL" monogram underlined by a small red rhythm-strip
  accent, like a heartbeat trace running beneath the wordmark.
- **motif**: ECG (accent), typographic
- **colors**: ink `#1A1A1A` letterforms, red `#CB3C33` accent trace.
- **register**: typographic
- **wordmark**: **includes text** ("HRL")
- **why_it_works**: The only wordmark in the set; the bold sans-serif
  survives small sizes on its own, with the pulse trace as a bonus flourish
  rather than a legibility dependency.
- **risk**: At 32px the underline trace all but disappears, leaving a plain
  "HRL" monogram with no HRV cue — the letters alone must carry it.

### b1ecg-08 — "Pulse Emitter"
- **concept**: A compact red R-peak spike broadcasting three concentric
  rhythm waves outward in purple, green, and blue — the beat *propagating*,
  per the brief's "pulse: concentric rings radiating" vocabulary.
- **motif**: ECG + pulse (radiating)
- **colors**: red `#CB3C33` spike, purple `#9558B2` / green `#389826` /
  blue `#4063D8` waves (radius-ordered, blue outermost as the sparing
  accent).
- **register**: scientific / illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Distinct silhouette from every "line in a container"
  mark in the set — reads as signal + transmission, which is exactly what
  HRV feature extraction *is*.
- **risk**: The off-center composition (spike left, arcs right) uses the
  canvas asymmetrically; at a glance it could be mistaken for a generic
  wifi/signal icon if the red spike doesn't register first.

### b1ecg-09 — "QRS Chevron"
- **concept**: The QRS complex rebuilt from pure straight-line chevrons —
  zero curves anywhere — split into three color segments (P/baseline,
  core QRS, T/baseline), the most geometric/angular mark in the set.
- **motif**: ECG
- **colors**: purple `#9558B2` (P + lead-in), red `#CB3C33` (QRS core),
  green `#389826` (T + lead-out).
- **register**: minimalist / geometric
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: The sharp miter joins and tri-color segmentation give
  it a distinct "logotype pictogram" personality versus the smoother,
  round-capped traces elsewhere in the set.
- **risk**: The all-angular P/T bumps are a stylization, not a literal ECG
  waveform — a purist could call the flattened P/T shapes less
  "scientifically honest" than the curved versions.

### b1ecg-10 — "Rhythm Loop"
- **concept**: One unbroken stroke rises into a sharp R-peak, then loops
  closed into a heart silhouette — the rhythm-strip literally becomes the
  heart's outline, with the spike standing in for the heart's cleft.
- **motif**: ECG enclosing a heart
- **colors**: linear gradient purple `#9558B2` → red `#CB3C33`.
- **register**: illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Satisfies the brief's "ECG line forming/enclosing a
  heart" prompt while staying ECG-first — it's one continuous trace, not a
  heart built from primitives, keeping it distinct from other studios'
  heart-glyph work.
- **risk**: Closest of the set to reading as "just a heart outline" rather
  than "ECG" if the small central notch doesn't register at a glance — the
  most heart-forward mark in an ECG-first lane.

---

## Strongest picks
1. **b1ecg-03 "Pulse Tile"** — the cleanest, most universally legible
   app-icon-grade mark; the safest default choice.
2. **b1ecg-05 "R-Peak Bolt"** — most distinctive silhouette, best
   favicon-scale performance, confident use of the blue accent.
3. **b1ecg-08 "Pulse Emitter"** — freshest concept in the set (rhythm as
   propagation), best differentiator from generic "heart monitor" clip art.
