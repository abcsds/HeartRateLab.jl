# B_hrv_first — Lane: Poincaré plots, attractors & phase-space (b2phase)

10 icon-only marks exploring the nonlinear-HRV signatures: the Poincaré ellipse,
limit cycles, attractors, spirals, and the SD1/SD2 axes. All use the Julia
palette (`#9558B2` purple, `#389826` green, `#CB3C33` red, `#4063D8` blue used
sparingly), 320×320 viewBox, transparent background, ~24px margin.

---

## b2phase-01 — "Poincaré Cloud"
- **concept**: The honest Poincaré plot — NN(n) vs NN(n+1) samples scattered
  inside an elongated ellipse laid on the 45° identity line. The most
  recognizable HRV signature, rendered as a clean logo instead of a plot.
- **motif**: Poincaré / attractor cloud
- **colors**: purple ellipse envelope (SD1/SD2 shape); scatter dots cycle
  red/green/blue/purple
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Instantly reads as "Poincaré plot" to any HRV researcher —
  it's the field's single most iconic chart, faithfully shaped.
- **risk**: at favicon size the scatter can read as a generic dotted blob
  before the ellipse silhouette registers.

## b2phase-02 — "SD Compass"
- **concept**: SD1 (short axis) and SD2 (long axis) — the two numbers every
  Poincaré analysis reports — abstracted into a caliper/compass cross on the
  diagonal, with a ghost ellipse and a mean-NN dot at the crossing.
- **motif**: Poincaré / SD1-SD2 axes
- **colors**: green = SD2 (long), purple = SD1 (short), red = mean-NN center dot
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Turns two abstract HRV metrics into an immediately legible
  crosshair mark — distinct from every other ellipse-based logo in the set.
- **risk**: without HRV context a viewer may just see "a green X," missing the
  SD1/SD2 reference — needs the surrounding brand story to land fully.

## b2phase-03 — "Limit Cycle"
- **concept**: A Van der Pol relaxation-oscillator limit cycle — the closed
  loop a nonlinear heartbeat model settles into — drawn as a bold stadium-
  shaped orbit with a beat (red dot) riding the trajectory.
- **motif**: attractor / limit cycle
- **colors**: purple-to-blue gradient stroke, red pulse dot
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: A closed loop is the simplest, boldest way to say
  "dynamical system in equilibrium" — reads clearly even at 32px.
- **risk**: without labeling, a stadium shape alone could be mistaken for a
  generic rounded-rectangle icon rather than "phase space."

## b2phase-04 — "Lorenz Wings"
- **concept**: The two-lobe topology of the Lorenz attractor (the package
  models Lorenz dynamics) stylized as one continuous infinity-shaped stroke
  crossing at the central saddle point.
- **motif**: attractor / Lorenz butterfly
- **colors**: purple-to-green gradient wings, red saddle-point dot
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Captures the defining "two wings, one crossing" structure
  of the Lorenz butterfly in a single elegant stroke instead of a busy
  trajectory dump.
- **risk**: could be read as a plain infinity/∞ symbol by viewers unfamiliar
  with the Lorenz attractor, losing the dynamical-systems reference.

## b2phase-05 — "Orbit Spiral"
- **concept**: A decaying spiral trajectory converging on a fixed point —
  the phase-space picture of a heart rhythm settling back to baseline after
  a perturbation (a stable focus, the counterpart to the limit cycle).
- **motif**: attractor / spiral orbit
- **colors**: purple-to-blue gradient spiral, red fixed-point dot
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: A converging spiral is a universally readable "settling
  down" shape, giving the set a counterpoint to the closed-loop marks.
- **risk**: at very small sizes the outer spiral arm can thin out and nearly
  disappear against a busy background.

## b2phase-06 — "Return-Map Heart"
- **concept**: The brief's suggested return-map scatter, deliberately arranged
  so the point cloud's silhouette reads as a heart — a knowing wink between
  "this is HRV data" and "this is a heart."
- **motif**: heart / return-map scatter
- **colors**: full cycle of purple/red/green/blue across the ten dots
- **register**: illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Delivers the heart glyph the brief calls for "constructed
  from dots" while staying inside this studio's data/attractor vocabulary.
- **risk**: the least "scientifically literal" mark in the set — a purist
  might object that no real return map looks like a heart; it's intentionally
  playful rather than a claimed empirical result.

## b2phase-07 — "Diagonal Trio"
- **concept**: Julia's three signature colors placed as three large dots
  directly on the Poincaré 45° identity line — a nod to the Julia three-dot
  identity that stays true to Direction B's rule (no forced triangle).
- **motif**: Poincaré / three-dot riff
- **colors**: purple, red, green in a row (Julia's three core colors)
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Bridges the Julia-ecosystem visual convention and the HRV
  phase-space lane in the simplest possible mark — three dots, one line.
- **risk**: without the diagonal tick marks for context it could look like a
  plain three-dot row rather than a phase-space reference.

## b2phase-08 — "Nested Cycles"
- **concept**: Two concentric orbits at very different scale — a big loose
  loop (green, high HRV / healthy variability) nested against a small tight
  loop (red, low HRV / stress) — a direct visual comparison of autonomic
  states in phase space.
- **motif**: attractor / nested limit cycles
- **colors**: green = healthy/high-variability orbit, red = low-variability
  orbit, darker shade dots mark orbit position
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: The size contrast alone tells the HRV story (bigger orbit
  = more variability) without needing any text.
- **risk**: could be misread as a plain target/bullseye icon if the HRV
  framing isn't provided alongside it.

## b2phase-09 — "Pulse Ellipses"
- **concept**: A heartbeat rippling outward along the Poincaré diagonal —
  three same-orientation ellipses of increasing size and decreasing opacity,
  like a pulse propagating through phase space.
- **motif**: Poincaré / pulse rings
- **colors**: red (innermost/loudest), purple, green (outermost/faintest)
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Merges the "pulse" and "Poincaré diagonal" vocabularies
  from the brief into a single ripple mark, distinct from the other
  ellipse-based logos by being a fading concentric family rather than a
  scatter or a cross.
- **risk**: the fainter outer rings can wash out at favicon size or on busy
  backgrounds.

## b2phase-10 — "Triangular Orbit"
- **concept**: One continuous, asymmetric closed trajectory (a limit cycle)
  that happens to visit three phase-space nodes in Julia's three colors —
  a dynamic, orbit-first alternative to the static Julia three-dot mark.
- **motif**: attractor / limit cycle with three-color nodes
- **colors**: purple, red, green nodes on an ink-colored orbit stroke
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Satisfies the Julia three-color convention while keeping
  the composition unmistakably "orbit," not "logo triangle" — the flowing
  asymmetric loop is the dominant shape, the dots are secondary accents.
- **risk**: the closest mark in the set to Julia's own three-dot logo; needs
  the connecting loop to stay bold so it doesn't collapse into "three dots in
  a triangle" at a glance.
