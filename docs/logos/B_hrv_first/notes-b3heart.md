# B3 — Abstract hearts, pulse rings & the inter-beat interval

Studio lane: Direction B (HRV-first), sub-lane "abstract hearts, pulse rings & the
inter-beat interval." Ten icon-forward SVGs (one wordmark) exploring geometric
hearts, radiating pulse rings, and the IBI gap as hero motifs — no cliché
valentine hearts, no forced Julia three-dot arrangement.

---

## b3heart-01 — Cleft Beat Heart
- **concept**: A two-tone geometric heart (purple/green) whose natural top
  cleft bursts into a small red QRS-style zigzag, fusing "heart" and
  "heartbeat" into one silhouette instead of pairing them side by side.
- **motif**: heart / ECG-spike accent
- **colors**: Julia purple + green as the two lobes (split down the center),
  Julia red for the spike — reads as three-color Julia identity without three dots.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the spike literally grows out of the heart's own anatomy,
  so the "beat" isn't decorative — it's structural.
- **risk**: at very small sizes the thin spike can soften into a short tick;
  it still survives because the two-tone heart alone reads fine.

## b3heart-02 — Loop Beat Heart
- **concept**: The heart outline is drawn as one continuous open stroke,
  purple on the left arc and green on the right, meeting at a red dot at the
  bottom point — a heart "traced" by a single pulse line rather than filled.
- **motif**: heart made of a looping line
- **colors**: purple + green stroke halves, red dot marks the beat/closure point.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: huge negative space (open, unfilled heart) reads
  immediately as heart while feeling like a signal trace, not clip-art.
- **risk**: relies on the viewer completing the shape from an open contour;
  works because the heart silhouette is unmistakable even unfilled.

## b3heart-03 — Sonar Pulse
- **concept**: A beat propagating outward as three thick concentric rings
  around a solid core, like sonar/radar from a single heartbeat origin.
- **motif**: pulse rings
- **colors**: purple (outer) → blue → green rings tightening toward a red
  core, using all four Julia colors deliberately, outer-to-inner.
- **register**: minimalist / scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: pure radial symmetry and bold ring widths make it the
  most instantly "pulse" of the set and hold up perfectly at favicon size.
- **risk**: without a literal heart or ECG shape, on its own it reads as
  "pulse/signal" more than "heart" — relies on rest of the mark family for context.

## b3heart-04 — Origin Pulse
- **concept**: A small solid heart at the base with two rising arcs above it,
  like sound/signal waves broadcasting upward from the heart itself.
- **motif**: heart + pulse rings (directional)
- **colors**: red heart, purple waves (single hue at two opacities to avoid
  a "rainbow" read).
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: directional (upward-only) arcs read as "broadcasting"
  rather than a generic badge, and keep the heart as the unambiguous focal shape.
- **risk**: if reproduced very small and monochrome, the waves can visually
  detach from the heart; mitigated by keeping the heart large relative to the arcs.

## b3heart-05 — IBI Caliper
- **concept**: Two R-peak spikes on a baseline with a double-headed arrow
  measuring the literal gap between them — the inter-beat interval as hero,
  drawn like a dimension line in a technical drawing.
- **motif**: IBI-gap
- **colors**: purple + green peaks, red measurement arrow (the "signal" color
  pulling focus to the interval itself).
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: this is literally what the package measures — nothing
  about it needs explaining to an HRV-literate viewer.
- **risk**: the dashed guide lines are the thinnest elements in the whole set;
  they hold at 32px but are the first thing to soften on print reproduction.

## b3heart-06 — Bracket Interval
- **concept**: Two beat-peaks framed by a hard right-angle bracket, like an
  engineering dimension bracket, with a red dot marking the measured span.
- **motif**: IBI-gap (brackets)
- **colors**: purple + green peaks, ink bracket, red accent dot.
- **register**: scientific / minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: distinct silhouette from the arrow (05) and caliper (07)
  variants — reads almost as a monogram/glyph at small sizes.
- **risk**: without color the bracket could be mistaken for a generic UI
  "group" icon; the two colored peaks anchor it back to "heartbeat."

## b3heart-07 — Caliper on a Beat
- **concept**: A literal drafting caliper — two legs from a pivot point —
  pinching the distance between two R-peaks on a baseline.
- **motif**: IBI-gap (caliper)
- **colors**: purple + green peaks, blue caliper legs, red pivot joint.
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: "measuring instrument touching a beat" is a very direct,
  unambiguous metaphor for what HRV analysis does.
- **risk**: the pivot-and-legs shape alone (without peaks) could read as a
  simple triangle/tent; the two colored peaks are what save the read.

## b3heart-08 — Faceted Heart
- **concept**: A low-poly heart built from three flat color facets (purple,
  green, red) meeting at a shared center point — a geometric, faceted
  reinterpretation of the heart silhouette instead of soft cliché curves.
- **motif**: abstract/geometric heart
- **colors**: purple + green top facets, red bottom facet — full three-color
  Julia trio, echoing the tri-color identity without three dots.
- **register**: minimalist / illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the sharp facet lines and flat color blocking make it
  feel deliberately engineered/scientific rather than decorative.
- **risk**: at a glance it can momentarily read as a shield or gem before
  "heart" resolves; the deep central notch and bottom point are what anchor it.

## b3heart-09 — Ripple Heart
- **concept**: A small solid red heart at the center with two larger nested
  heart-shaped outlines radiating outward, like a heartbeat's ripples keeping
  the shape of their source.
- **motif**: heart + pulse rings (nested)
- **colors**: red solid core, green + blue outlines fading in opacity outward.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: fuses two lane motifs (heart, pulse rings) into a single
  shape family instead of bolting them together, unlike 04's heart+arcs pairing.
- **risk**: the outer blue ring is the softest element in the set by design
  (it's a ripple, meant to fade) — still visible but intentionally the
  least dominant stroke.

## b3heart-10 — HeartRateLab Wordmark
- **concept**: A heart pierced by its own ECG trace (the line dips and
  spikes exactly through the heart's center), paired with the bold
  "HeartRateLab" wordmark below.
- **motif**: heart / ECG-spike
- **colors**: green-tinted heart outline in purple, red ECG line, ink text.
- **register**: typographic
- **wordmark**: includes text ("HeartRateLab")
- **why_it_works**: the only text-bearing mark in the set — reserved for
  contexts (README header, docs site) where a fully spelled-out name is
  wanted; the icon alone still reads as heart+pulse if cropped.
- **risk**: the text will not be legible at 32px (inherent to any wordmark
  at favicon size); the icon portion was kept bold enough to survive alone
  as a fallback favicon crop.

---

## Strongest picks
1. **b3heart-02 (Loop Beat Heart)** — the cleanest, most original interpretation
   of "heart made of a looping line"; huge negative space, no clutter.
2. **b3heart-03 (Sonar Pulse)** — the boldest, most favicon-proof mark in the
   set; unmistakable "pulse" read at any size.
3. **b3heart-08 (Faceted Heart)** — the most distinctive take on the abstract
   heart, ties back to Julia's tri-color identity without copying the dots.
