# Studio A2 — "Return maps, orbits & phase-space, keeping the triangle"

Direction A (Julia-Native), lane: return maps / Poincaré plots / limit cycles / orbital motion,
with the three Julia dots always remaining the recognizable hero triad.

---

## a2return-01 — "Limit-Cycle Ring"
- **concept**: A thin closed orbit loop threads through the three Julia dots as if they were
  bodies riding a stable limit cycle; a small arrowhead marks the direction of travel.
- **motif**: attractor / three-dot / orbit
- **colors**: Julia purple, green, red (full triad); ink-grey ring, low opacity so the dots stay hero.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: One continuous stroke instantly reads as "orbit," and the triad sits exactly
  where the Julia mark expects it — no conflict between the two readings.
- **risk**: The directional arrowhead is subtle and may vanish at 16px; the ring alone still carries
  the concept if it does.

## a2return-02 — "Poincaré Diagonal"
- **concept**: The classic upright Julia triangle sits inside a faint 45°-tilted ellipse cloud with
  axes and an identity-line diagonal behind it — a Poincaré return-map backdrop framing the triad
  without disturbing its familiar silhouette.
- **motif**: Poincaré / three-dot
- **colors**: full triad plus purple-tinted ellipse fill/stroke for the SD1/SD2 cloud.
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Keeps the Julia triangle 100% intact (safest identity read) while layering in
  genuine HRV semantics (axes + diagonal + elongated cloud) as background texture.
- **risk**: At favicon size the axes/diagonal can disappear, leaving "just the triangle" — still
  fine as a fallback, but the Poincaré reference is lost.

## a2return-03 — "Van der Pol Eye"
- **concept**: A pinched, flattened loop (echoing a relaxation-oscillator limit cycle rather than a
  plain circle) with the triad riding its rim — purple at the narrow top, red/green at the wide
  bottom shoulders.
- **motif**: attractor / three-dot
- **colors**: full triad; ink-grey loop.
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: The visible waist pinch differentiates it immediately from a generic ring
  (01/08), giving a distinct "nonlinear oscillator" read for viewers who know the shape.
- **risk**: Viewers unfamiliar with Van der Pol cycles may just see "a slightly irregular blob";
  it still reads as orbit + triad even without that prior knowledge.

## a2return-04 — "Trail Triad"
- **concept**: Each dot trails a short comet-tail arc, all curling the same rotational direction,
  implying the three dots are in synchronized orbital motion around a shared center.
- **motif**: attractor / three-dot
- **colors**: full triad; trails tinted at low opacity in each dot's own color.
- **register**: illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Motion trails are an intuitive, low-cognitive-load way to say "orbit" without
  needing a full ring, and it's visually the most dynamic mark in the set.
- **risk**: At very small sizes the pale trails can read as smudges/shadows rather than motion —
  mitigated by keeping the dots themselves crisp and full-opacity.

## a2return-05 — "Spiral Converge"
- **concept**: A spiral trajectory winds outward from center and settles onto the top (purple) dot,
  visualizing a phase-space path converging onto a limit cycle.
- **motif**: attractor
- **colors**: full triad; ink-grey spiral.
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Directly illustrates the "converging to a limit cycle" dynamical-systems idea
  named in the brief, distinguishing it from the pure-ring marks.
- **risk**: The spiral's inner windings are the most delicate linework in the set and are the first
  thing to blur away below ~24px; the triad remains legible regardless.

## a2return-06 — "Pulse Orbit"
- **concept**: The limit-cycle ring itself doubles as a heartbeat: its lower arc, between the red
  and green dots, interrupts into a small QRS spike — orbit and pulse fused into one line.
  built from two path segments: the plain upper ring and the beat-shaped lower ring.
- **motif**: ECG / attractor / three-dot
- **colors**: full triad; ink-grey ring/spike.
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Single most literal fusion of "HRV" (the spike) and "orbit/phase-space" (the
  ring) in the set — reads as both at once rather than either/or.
- **risk**: The QRS spike is small relative to the ring and is the one element likeliest to soften
  into a blur at 32px; the ring + triad still carries the mark on its own.

## a2return-07 — "Return Map Minimal"
- **concept**: A true, minimal Poincaré return-map: bottom-left axes, a dashed identity diagonal,
  a lean elongated SD1/SD2 cloud, and exactly three dots stretched along the diagonal — still
  colored and weighted as the Julia triad, just laid out scientifically instead of upright.
- **motif**: Poincaré / three-dot
- **colors**: full triad; Julia blue used sparingly for the cloud outline (the brief's accent color).
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: The most scientifically honest Poincaré plot in the set (real elongated cloud
  on the diagonal, exactly the shape described in the brief) while still reading as three Julia dots.
- **risk**: Because the dots are diagonally arranged rather than the iconic "one over two," a viewer
  who doesn't already know the Julia mark could parse it as "just a scatter plot" first.

## a2return-08 — "Concentric Orbit"
- **concept**: Pulse rings ripple outward from a shared center; the triad rides the outermost ring,
  reading simultaneously as "orbit" and "beat propagating outward."
- **motif**: pulse / attractor / three-dot
- **colors**: full triad; ink-grey concentric rings at graduated opacity.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: Cleanest, most symmetric mark in the set — concentric rings are extremely
  robust at small sizes and the triad sits confidently on top.
- **risk**: The two inner rings are the most decorative/least essential element and could be
  dropped without hurting the concept — a sign they're doing relatively little work.

## a2return-09 — "Linked Orbits"
- **concept**: Two crossing orbit loops, stacked vertically: purple rides the small upper loop,
  red and green ride the wide lower loop — a coupled-oscillator read while preserving "one dot
  above, two below."
- **motif**: attractor / three-dot
- **colors**: full triad; ink-grey rings.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: The only mark in the set built from two distinct orbits rather than one,
  giving genuine variety while an explicit top/bottom split keeps the Julia hierarchy legible.
- **risk**: The overlapping rings could be mistaken for a Venn diagram rather than "orbits" without
  the rest of the set's context to anchor the phase-space reading.

## a2return-10 — "Wordmark Orbit"
- **concept**: A compact orbit ring and triad icon sits above a bold "HRL" monogram — the one
  wordmark entry in this lane.
- **motif**: attractor / three-dot
- **colors**: full triad; ink-grey ring and text.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: includes text ("HRL")
- **why_it_works**: Gives the set a lockup option for contexts (README headers, docs banners)
  where a bare icon needs a name attached, using a bold web-safe font so it stays self-contained.
- **risk**: "HRL" is the element most likely to soften into an unreadable smudge at 16–32px; the
  icon above it should be treated as the true favicon crop if that size is needed.

---

## Strongest picks
1. **a2return-01 "Limit-Cycle Ring"** — cleanest, most immediate "orbit + Julia triad" read.
2. **a2return-08 "Concentric Orbit"** — most robust at tiny sizes, elegant pulse+orbit fusion.
3. **a2return-06 "Pulse Orbit"** — most literal HRV+phase-space fusion (ring that beats).
