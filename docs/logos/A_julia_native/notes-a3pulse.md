# Studio A3 — "Minimal geometry, pulse & negative space"

Lane: purest, most reductive riffs on the Julia three-dot mark, woven with HRV
meaning through pulse rings, negative space, and dot geometry rather than
literal ECG line-art. Icon-only, flat, favicon-first.

---

### a3pulse-01 — "Triad Pulse"
- **concept**: All three Julia dots emit a synchronous pulse ring outward at once, like three hearts beating in phase — the simplest possible reading of "pulse."
- **motif**: pulse / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2 (top), red #CB3C33 (bottom-left), green #389826 (bottom-right) — canonical Julia triad, each ringed in its own color.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: instantly reads as both "Julia" (three colored dots, canonical triangle) and "pulse" (concentric rings) with zero extra elements.
- **risk**: the outer, faintest ring may thin out to near-invisible at 32px; core dots + first ring still carry it.

### a3pulse-02 — "Sinus Spark"
- **concept**: pulse rings radiate from the purple dot alone, casting it as the "pacemaker" (sinus node) that fires first and drives the other two into rhythm.
- **motif**: pulse / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2 dominant with rings; red #CB3C33 and green #389826 calm and small.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: asymmetric energy source gives the mark a clear focal point and a distinct silhouette from a3pulse-01 despite sharing the ring device.
- **risk**: a viewer unfamiliar with sinus-node physiology may just read "one big dot, two small dots" without the pacemaker nuance — the Julia triad and pulse still land regardless.

### a3pulse-03 — "Heart of Three"
- **concept**: a thin heart-shaped outline threads through the three dots — purple and green sit at the two lobes, red sits at the bottom point — so the heart is drawn *by* the Julia triad rather than added beside it.
- **motif**: heart / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2, green #389826 at the lobes; red #CB3C33 at the point (echoes "R" for R-peak at the apex); ink #1A1A1A stroke at low opacity for the outline.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the heart silhouette is unmistakable even though it's built from only three dots and a thin stroke — very little ink, high recognition.
- **risk**: breaks the canonical "one on top, two below" Julia arrangement (here it's two-up/one-down to read as a heart); still uses all three official colors and the circle motif so Julia identity holds.

### a3pulse-04 — "Heart Cut"
- **concept**: the canonical Julia triad, tightly overlapping, with a small heart shape masked out of the negative space exactly where all three dots meet — the heart is hidden inside the Julia identity rather than drawn on top of it.
- **motif**: heart / negative-space / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2, red #CB3C33, green #389826, full canonical arrangement.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: rewards a closer look — first read is "three big Julia dots," second read reveals the heart cut into the overlap, satisfying the brief's "subtle" heart directive.
- **risk**: at 32px the tiny heart cutout can shrink to a faint dot/highlight rather than a legible heart — the mark still reads perfectly as "Julia" at that size, but the heart easter egg is easy to lose.

### a3pulse-05 — "R-Spike"
- **concept**: the purple dot grows a narrow triangular spike off its crown, turning the dot itself into the silhouette of an ECG R-peak.
- **motif**: ECG / R-peak / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2 (spiked dot), red #CB3C33 and green #389826 plain below.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: a single dot doing double duty as both "Julia purple" and "R-peak" is about as reductive as this idea can get — one clean silhouette, no extra strokes.
- **risk**: at small sizes the spike could soften into looking like a flame or teardrop rather than an explicit ECG reference; kept narrow and tall specifically to resist that reading.

### a3pulse-06 — "Notch"
- **concept**: a bold, narrow V is cut into the crown of the red dot — a QRS notch carved directly into the "R" (for R-peak) colored dot, in negative space rather than added line-art.
- **motif**: ECG / negative-space / three-dot
- **colors**: red #CB3C33 (notched), purple #9558B2 and green #389826 plain.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: subtractive rather than additive — keeps the silhouette as three plain circles at a glance, with the notch rewarding closer inspection, consistent with the lane's "negative space" mandate.
- **risk**: if the notch is rendered too wide it can misread as a Pac-Man bite; kept intentionally thin and deep to avoid that — worth a final check against very small renders.

### a3pulse-07 — "Groove"
- **concept**: the three dots are fused into a single row and a full PQRST zigzag channel is cut straight through them as a bright negative-space groove — the "beat" is quite literally carved through the Julia identity.
- **motif**: ECG / negative-space / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2, red #CB3C33, green #389826 side by side.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the strongest, most literal ECG reference in the set while remaining pure negative space (no drawn line, only a cut) — scientifically honest QRS shape.
- **risk**: the row layout departs furthest from the canonical triangle arrangement; the three named Julia colors still carry the identity.

### a3pulse-08 — "Ring Rhythm"
- **concept**: the three dots are drawn as open rings rather than filled circles, with a small pulse line — in Julia blue, used sparingly — threading the gap between them like a beat passing through.
- **motif**: pulse / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2, red #CB3C33, green #389826 as rings; blue #4063D8 for the connecting pulse line (the brief's "use sparingly" accent).
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: outline dots + a wired pulse line is a genuinely different figure-ground treatment from every other mark in the set (all others use solid fills).
- **risk**: thin ring strokes and the small blue squiggle are the most detail-dense mark here; verified they still separate cleanly at 32px, but they have the least margin for further shrinking.

### a3pulse-09 — "Crescendo"
- **concept**: the three dots grow in size along a rising diagonal, staging a visual crescendo — the beat builds from small to large, left to right.
- **motif**: graduated-size / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2 (smallest), red #CB3C33 (medium), green #389826 (largest), joined by a faint guide line.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: pure size progression communicates "building rhythm" without any line art or ECG iconography at all — the most abstract, gallery-clean mark in the set.
- **risk**: without prior context, "growing dots" reads more as generic motion/growth than specifically "heartbeat" — leans on the other 9 marks in the set to anchor the HRV theme.

### a3pulse-10 — "Beat Steps"
- **concept**: the three dots sit at low/high/low positions along a baseline — literally sketching a single beat's silhouette through dot placement alone, with the red (R) dot at the peak.
- **motif**: ECG / graduated-position / three-dot
- **colors**: purple #9558B2 and green #389826 low and flanking; red #CB3C33 at the peak.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: encodes the heartbeat waveform in the dots' Y-position rather than an added line, keeping the mark reductive while still scientifically plausible as a simplified beat.
- **risk**: could be misread as a plain triangular node graph rather than a heartbeat if seen without the connecting baseline stroke, which is why the stroke is kept (faint but present).

---

## Strongest picks
- **a3pulse-07 "Groove"** — the cleanest, most scientifically literal ECG read in the whole lane while staying pure negative space.
- **a3pulse-01 "Triad Pulse"** — the most immediately legible "Julia + pulse" combination, works at any size.
- **a3pulse-03 "Heart of Three"** — best surprise/delight ratio; a heart appears from just three dots and a hairline stroke.
