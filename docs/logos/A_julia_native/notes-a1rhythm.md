# Studio A1 — "Rhythm Strips & Beats-on-a-Line" — notes

Direction A (Julia-Native), lane: dots as beats/events riding ECG rhythm lines,
tachograms, or IBI tick-marks. All 10 logos below keep the three Julia dots
(purple `#9558B2`, red `#CB3C33`, green `#389826`) as the load-bearing identity
element; blue `#4063D8` was not needed in this lane.

---

## a1rhythm-01 — "Rhythm Strip Classic"
- **concept**: Three P-QRS-T complexes on a running ECG baseline; each R-peak
  tip is capped by a Julia dot, so the three dots literally *are* the
  heartbeats.
- **motif**: ECG / three-dot
- **colors**: purple, red, green dots; ink `#1A1A1A` trace
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: most literal fusion of "Julia dots" + "heartbeat" — instantly reads both ways.
- **risk**: closest in spirit to a generic ECG logo; differentiation relies entirely on the exact dot placement and QRST detailing.

## a1rhythm-02 — "Interval Calipers"
- **concept**: Three beats sit directly on a rhythm line like beads on a
  string; unequal double-arrow brackets beneath measure the gap between
  beats, visualizing HRV as literally *unequal inter-beat intervals*.
- **motif**: IBI-gap / three-dot
- **colors**: purple, red, green beads; ink calipers
- **register**: scientific / diagrammatic
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: directly visualizes the package's core measurement (the ⟷ interval), which no other logo in the set does as explicitly.
- **risk**: the caliper arrows are the smallest detail in the set and soften first under heavy downscaling/blur (still legible at 32px in testing, but least robust).

## a1rhythm-03 — "Tachogram Steps"
- **concept**: A beat-to-beat interval series (tachogram) drawn as a jagged
  polyline; three of five vertices are the colored Julia dots, two are plain
  gray anchors, reading as a real scientific time series rather than a
  decorative triangle.
- **motif**: tachogram / three-dot
- **colors**: purple, red, green on the three "named" beats; neutral gray for filler points
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the zigzag height literally encodes IBI variability, which is exactly what a tachogram is.
- **risk**: could be mistaken for a generic line-chart icon if viewed without any HRV context.

## a1rhythm-04 — "Pulse Ring"
- **concept**: The rhythm strip bent into a closed loop — a recurring cardiac
  cycle — with clock-like tick marks around the rim and the three Julia dots
  at top/lower-left/lower-right marking beats around the ring.
- **motif**: pulse / limit-cycle / three-dot
- **colors**: purple, red, green dots; faint ink ring and ticks
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: only circular composition in the set, giving strong differentiation and nodding to the brief's "limit cycle" nonlinear-dynamics vocabulary.
- **risk**: the weakest at reading "heartbeat" on first glance — could look like a plain wheel/clock without the tick detail; leans more "Julia mark in a circle" than "HRV."

## a1rhythm-05 — "IBI Comb"
- **concept**: A row of vertical tick marks with deliberately irregular
  spacing (like an RR-interval comb/actigraphy strip); three thicker ticks
  are capped with the Julia dots, the rest are plain gray.
- **motif**: IBI tick-marks / three-dot
- **colors**: purple, red, green dot-caps; gray filler ticks
- **register**: scientific / minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: irregular tick spacing is scientifically honest shorthand for "variability," the core HRV concept, expressed with almost no clutter.
- **risk**: at very small sizes the thin gray filler ticks can visually recede entirely, leaving only 3 dots-on-sticks — still legible, but loses some of the "comb" reading.

## a1rhythm-06 — "Rising Rhythm"
- **concept**: Three dots climb a rising line (heart rate accelerating) while
  tick marks beneath compress rightward, showing IBI shortening as HR rises —
  a trend view, distinct from the variability views elsewhere in the set.
- **motif**: tachogram (trend) / three-dot
- **colors**: purple, red, green along the ascent
- **register**: minimalist / scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: ties two real HRV facts together (rising HR = shortening IBI) in one uncluttered diagonal composition.
- **risk**: without the compressing ticks it would just be "three dots on a diagonal"; the ticks are essential and small.

## a1rhythm-07 — "Breath Wave"
- **concept**: A slow, smooth sinusoid (respiratory sinus arrhythmia — the
  breathing modulation of heart rate) carries three beats at crest, midline,
  and trough, in contrast to the spiky ECG pieces elsewhere in the set.
- **motif**: pulse (modulation) / three-dot
- **colors**: purple and green at the crests, red at the trough
- **register**: illustrative / organic
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the only soft/organic register in the set, giving the collection range while staying scientifically grounded in RSA.
- **risk**: on its own (without ECG siblings nearby) a smooth wave alone reads more "signal/audio" than "heart" — relies on dot color identity to anchor it as Julia/HRV.

## a1rhythm-08 — "Tachogram Capsules"
- **concept**: Five rounded-capsule bars of varying height stand for a run of
  inter-beat intervals; three are colored Julia capsules (tall = long IBI),
  two flanking ones are neutral gray, directly matching the brief's
  "dot-heights encoding a tachogram" idea.
- **motif**: tachogram (bar) / three-dot (elongated)
- **colors**: purple, red, green capsules; gray flanking bars
- **register**: scientific / graphic
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: boldest, most poster-friendly mark in the set — reads instantly even at a glance.
- **risk**: of everything in the set this is the one most likely to be mistaken for a generic bar-chart/analytics icon rather than specifically "heartbeat."

## a1rhythm-09 — "Monitor Grid"
- **concept**: A bedside-cardiac-monitor bezel with a faint graph-paper grid
  behind a small ECG trace; the three R-peaks are the Julia dots, giving the
  whole set a clinical, instrument-panel register.
- **motif**: ECG / clinical monitor / three-dot
- **colors**: purple, red, green peaks; ink bezel and grid
- **register**: scientific / illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the rounded bezel + grid instantly signals "clinical monitor," adding context the plain rhythm strip (01) doesn't have.
- **risk**: most detail-dense mark in the set; at 32px the grid all but disappears (by design, it's meant to fade), leaving essentially logo 01's trace in a rounded box — acceptable but worth knowing.

## a1rhythm-10 — "HRL Monogram Strip"
- **concept**: A tiny beads-on-a-line rhythm icon (purple/red/green, red
  raised in the classic top-dot position) sits above a bold "HRL" monogram —
  the one wordmark logo in the set.
- **motif**: three-dot / IBI-gap (icon) + monogram
- **colors**: purple, red, green dots; ink text
- **register**: typographic
- **wordmark**: includes text ("HRL")
- **why_it_works**: pairs the three-dot identity with a short, bold, favicon-safe monogram — legible at 32px where a full "HeartRateLab" wordmark would not be.
- **risk**: as an initialism, "HRL" needs the accompanying icon or a caption elsewhere to disambiguate from unrelated acronyms.
