The comb, shrunk to an icon

Top 1 put the comb under the word. Top 2 takes that same comb, drops the gray ticks, keeps the three colour ticks + circles, compresses them horizontally (gap = our margin m), and sets the result as an icon to the left of the wordmark — all on the solved ratios (Ø:m:tick), the gray rail, JuliaMono Black. Hover a card to light up each rule on the mark.

Circle Ø435

Icon circle, sized so the whole mark is exactly the word's cap-height. Carries the same Ø:m:tick ratio as the big comb.

Margin m96

The one gap that rules everything: circle→tick-tip vertically AND circle→circle horizontally. Shrinking the comb = setting the horizontal gap to m.

Tick length203

Ticks follow a fixed tachogram — each dot's height is one interval. The tallest (dot on the cap line) = 0.47·Ø, from the comb's tallest tooth; a dot always sits m above its tip.

Spacing531

Centre-to-centre = Ø + m. The three colour ticks — spread across the word in top 1 — compressed to adjacent cells here.

Rail gray#8A8F98

The gray ticks are gone; only the short rail stays gray (3.3:1). All three ticks now carry a Julia colour.

Gap to word320

Icon right edge to the word's left ink. Icon vertically spans cap-line→baseline, so it locks to the type.

01 · Where it came from

Comb → drop grays → shrink to m → move left

The shrink rule. In top 1 the three colour ticks sat at the letters' optical centres (~2400–3000 apart). Here they collapse to adjacent cells, gap = m — the same margin that separates circle from tick and circle from rail. One constant m governs every gap in the mark. The rail stays the #8A8F98 gray (3.3:1); only the ticks are coloured now.
02 · Colour combinations

Where the colour goes

Two switches — letters {ink · coloured} × icon ticks {colour · gray (dots only)}. The three circles stay the Julia anchor in all four:

ink letters · colour ticks
ink letters · gray ticks (dots only)
coloured letters · colour ticks
coloured letters · gray ticks (dots only)

Ink letters + colour ticks keeps the icon as the only colour event — cleanest for an icon-left lockup; colouring the caps too makes the word echo the icon (bolder, busier).

03 · My picks

Top 1 & Top 2

Tick heights are fixed to the tachogram in both — the three dots read as three inter-beat intervals. The picks differ only in how far the colour spreads:

TOP 1Ink & anchored

Ink letters · colour ticks · tachogram heights · gray rail (#8A8F98). The icon is the single colour event; the word stays calm and legible. My recommendation for the icon-left lockup — it shrinks cleanly to a favicon.

Coloured caps · colour ticks · tachogram heights · lighter rail (#9AA0A6). The H·R·L caps echo the icon's colours — bolder and more expressive, better for hero/marketing than dense body use.
