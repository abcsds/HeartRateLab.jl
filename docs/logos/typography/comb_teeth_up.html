01 · The comb gray

How gray is it?

The series-2 comb teeth are #8A8F98 — a cool blue-gray (RGB 138·143·152), luminance 0.27, about 3.3 : 1 on white. Deliberately recessive: the comb underlines, it doesn't compete with the word or the dots. Alternatives, each on the tachogram comb:

02 · Colour combinations

Where the colour goes — every combination

In the original, only the accent tooth + ball carry colour; the letters stay ink. Two independent switches — letter {ink · coloured} and accent tooth {gray · coloured} — give four marks (the letter-wide circle stays the Julia anchor in all):

ink letters · gray accent tooth
ink letters · coloured accent tooth
coloured letters · gray accent tooth
coloured letters · coloured accent tooth

Colouring both letters and teeth doubles the colour and can feel loud; ink letters + coloured teeth keeps the word calm and lets the triad lead.

03 · Tooth-height pattern

Why are the teeth uneven? — it's a tachogram

In the original the heights are hand-set and irregular. Measured, the three coloured teeth are the tallest (34·32·34) and the grays wander 12–28. Read correctly, the tick heights are successive inter-beat intervals — their unevenness is the HRV the package measures. Four principled laws:

Flat

every gray tick equal — a plain rule; the three accents still lead.

Accent-only

gray ticks short & uniform, accents tall — maximum hierarchy, minimum noise.

RSA sine

heights follow a smooth sine — respiratory sinus arrhythmia, HR rising & falling with the breath.

Tachogram

heights = successive inter-beat intervals (HF+LF) — irregular; the variation IS the HRV.
