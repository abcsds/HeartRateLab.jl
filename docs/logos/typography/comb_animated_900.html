HRL Ring · series 3 · the ball ORBITS the ring and taps the centred letters

On the round mark the ball can't travel a line — it orbits the ring, hitting the three beats at their unequal clock-spacings (150°·120°·90° — the same rhythm), and each pass taps the matching centred letter (colour + squash). Because the ball always stays on the ring, the colour variants are the remote-squash behaviour by nature.
