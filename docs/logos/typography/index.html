HeartRateLab.jl · brand · design process

Three heartbeats, three dots, one word

HeartRateLab in JuliaMono Black (900), the three capitals tinted in the Julia palette. Below is the primary lockup — comb-under — with every guideline drawn on it: hover a parameter card to light it up on the mark.

Hover a card → the matching guide is highlighted on the logo (everything else dims). All values in em units (font-size 1000).

Ball Ø600

Each ball is one letter wide — the 600 monospace advance (0.82·cap) — floating below its capital.

Equal marginsm = 132

The gap letter→ball equals ball→comb line — both 132 (0.18·cap). The ball floats centred in the band.

Optical centres305·3313·5705

Ball, tick and capital share one vertical axis — each capital's ink centre, not its 600-cell centre.

The comb12 × 600

A gray IBI-tick rail under the word — one tick per letter, 600 apart; the three at H·R·L are tinted.

Julia palettepurple·red·green

H #9558B2, R #CB3C33, L #389826 — three of the four brand hues; blue stays reserved.

Rhythm5 : 4

Cell-centre hops 3000 : 2400 = exactly 5:4 (optical ≈1.26) — a swung triplet, the cadence the package measures.
