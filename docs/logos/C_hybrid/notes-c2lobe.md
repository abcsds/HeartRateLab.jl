# C_hybrid — lane: "heart/pulse built from three colored lobes/dots" (c2lobe series)

10 icon-only SVGs. Each fuses the Julia three-color triad into a single heart/pulse
silhouette by literally constructing the mark's lobes, wedges, petals, or arcs from
the three Julia colors — never an ECG trace with dots stuck on top (that's another
studio's lane).

---

## c2lobe-01 — Tri-Slice Heart
- **concept**: A solid heart silhouette cut into three Mercedes-badge-style 120°
  wedges radiating from its centroid — the Julia triad isn't decorating the heart,
  it *is* the heart, sliced like a pie.
- **motif**: heart / three-dot (reinterpreted as three wedges)
- **colors**: purple (upper-right wedge), red (bottom wedge, carries the point),
  green (upper-left wedge) — full saturation, no tints.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: instant heart read from the outline; the straight radial cuts
  read as intentional/geometric rather than accidental, and stay crisp at any size.
- **risk**: the hard straight seams are less "organic" than a heart typically feels;
  could scan as a piece of pie art before heart on first glance for some viewers.

## c2lobe-02 — Venn Pulse Heart
- **concept**: Three translucent circles in the classic Julia triangle arrangement
  overlap inside a heart clip; their alpha-blended intersections (a nod to the
  package's statistical/Venn heritage) fill the heart with a gradient of blended hue.
- **motif**: heart / three-dot (as overlapping Venn circles)
- **colors**: purple, green, red circles at ~0.78 opacity; overlaps blend toward
  warmer/darker mixed tones.
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the overlap/blend is a subtle wink at statistical Venn diagrams,
  appropriate for a data-analysis package, while the heart clip guarantees legible
  silhouette.
- **risk**: at very small sizes the blended overlap zones can read a touch muddy
  rather than three distinct colors.

## c2lobe-03 — Trefoil Pulse
- **concept**: Three dot-derived teardrop petals meet at a small blue pulse-core,
  rotated 120° apart — a three-fold rotationally symmetric "beat" flower rather than
  a literal heart outline.
- **motif**: pulse / three-dot (as rotational petals)
- **colors**: purple, green, red petals; blue accent core (used sparingly, per spec).
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: clean, original silhouette that is unmistakably built from three
  equal colored parts meeting at a single beating point.
- **risk**: reads clearly as "pulse/trefoil" but is the weakest of the ten for a
  literal "heart" read — it's a flower/knot more than a heart.

## c2lobe-04 — Three-Stroke Heart
- **concept**: The heart is drawn only as an outline, stitched from three thick
  rounded brush-stroke arcs in the three Julia colors — no fill, all negative space.
- **motif**: heart / IBI-gap (open interior reads as rhythm/negative-space)
- **colors**: purple (upper-left arc), green (upper-right arc), red (long bottom
  arc through the point).
- **register**: illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: confident use of negative space, very legible line-art heart
  that still telegraphs "three Julia colors" clearly.
- **risk**: thin strokes could weaken toward the two short top arcs at extreme
  small sizes if reproduced too thin; keep stroke-width generous.

## c2lobe-05 — Orbiting Beat
- **concept**: A small solid ink heart sits at the center while the three Julia
  dots orbit it on a thin ring, like electrons around a nucleus — the "package" is
  the heart, the "Julia identity" visibly orbits it.
- **motif**: heart / three-dot (orbital)
- **colors**: purple, green, red orbiting dots; ink heart core; faint ink orbit ring.
- **register**: illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: distinct atomic/orbital framing not used anywhere else in the
  set; the heart core stays legible even if the thin orbit ring vanishes at
  favicon size.
- **risk**: the orbit ring and dots are the smallest elements in the whole set —
  if reproduced under 32px on a busy background they could lose contrast.

## c2lobe-06 — Negative Heart
- **concept**: Three large solid Julia-colored circles fuse into a clover badge;
  a heart shape is punched out of the middle as pure negative space, with a thin
  ink line tracing the cut for crispness.
- **motif**: heart (negative space) / three-dot
- **colors**: purple, green, red badge circles; thin ink heart outline.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the cleverest use of negative space in the set — the heart is
  implied rather than drawn, and the outline guarantees it still reads instantly.
- **risk**: viewers scanning fast may first parse "three overlapping circles"
  before parsing the heart-shaped hole; the outline mitigates but doesn't eliminate.

## c2lobe-07 — Petal Heart
- **concept**: Three dot-derived teardrop petals — purple and green as the top
  lobes, red as the point — overlap generously so their union reads as one
  continuous heart rather than three separate drops.
- **motif**: heart / three-dot (as overlapping petals)
- **colors**: purple (left lobe), green (right lobe), red (point, visible as a
  seam through the cleft and the tip).
- **register**: illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: softer, more organic heart silhouette than the wedge/venn
  versions, while still obviously built from three separate colored parts.
- **risk**: the red petal is mostly hidden behind purple/green, showing only as a
  thin sliver — could be missed as a "third color" at a glance.

## c2lobe-08 — Dot-Fused Heart
- **concept**: The most literal reading of the brief — two big Julia dots (purple,
  green) sit exactly where a heart's two lobes belong, fused together, with red
  filling the residual point beneath them inside a single heart silhouette.
- **motif**: heart / three-dot (dots as literal lobes)
- **colors**: purple + green as the two lobes/dots, red as the base/point.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the strongest, cleanest fusion in the set — reads as heart AND
  as "two Julia dots" simultaneously, with rounded organic seams (not straight
  cuts) that echo real circle edges.
- **risk**: very low risk; the main caution is keeping the ink outline crisp at
  small export sizes so the red sliver at the bottom doesn't get lost.

## c2lobe-09 — Radiating Pulse Heart
- **concept**: Three nested heart contours, scaling down and filling in toward a
  solid red core, ripple outward like a pulse radiating from a beat — reshaping the
  HRV "concentric rings" motif into hearts instead of circles.
- **motif**: heart / pulse (concentric rings, heart-shaped)
- **colors**: purple (outer ring), green (middle ring), red (solid inner heart/core).
- **register**: scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: original combination of two vocabulary items from the brief
  (pulse rings + heart glyph) that neither reads as generic nor as a cliché
  valentine.
- **risk**: the outer purple ring is the thinnest, faintest element — needs a
  reasonably high-contrast background to stay visible at favicon size.

## c2lobe-10 — Pulse Turbine
- **concept**: Three thick colored arcs spin around a small dark core like a
  turbine/pinwheel — "three colored arcs sweeping into a pulse," read as a beat
  radiating outward in motion rather than as a heart outline.
- **motif**: pulse (arc-sweep) / three-dot (as arcs)
- **colors**: purple, green, red arcs; ink core.
- **register**: minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: bold, dynamic, unmistakably three-colored, and distinct in
  silhouette from every heart-shaped mark in the set — reads clearly as "pulse in
  motion."
- **risk**: reads as "pulse/energy wheel," not literally as a heart — leans on the
  brief's "or a beat" allowance rather than the heart silhouette.

---

## Strongest picks
1. **c2lobe-08** (Dot-Fused Heart) — the cleanest, most literal fusion; best
   single ambassador for the lane.
2. **c2lobe-01** (Tri-Slice Heart) — bold, geometric, instantly legible at any size.
3. **c2lobe-09** (Radiating Pulse Heart) — most original combination of HRV pulse
   vocabulary with the heart glyph.
