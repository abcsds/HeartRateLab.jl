# Direction C — Hybrid/Fusion — Studio "c1trace"

Lane: HRV artifacts that resolve INTO the three Julia dots — ECG traces, Poincaré
clusters, tachograms, attractors, spirals and networks whose structure literally
*is* the triad, so "Julia package" and "heartbeat/rhythm" read simultaneously.

---

### c1trace-01 — "Triad Pulse"
- **concept**: A single ECG baseline throws three QRS-style spikes of different
  heights and positions — short/short/tall — so the spike apexes trace the exact
  silhouette of the Julia triangle (two low outer dots, one tall centered dot).
  The heartbeat waveform IS the triangle.
- **motif**: ECG / three-dot
- **colors**: red + green (base spikes, Julia positions), purple (tall center spike)
- **register**: minimalist, scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: one continuous line does double duty — it's simultaneously a
  legible QRS complex and the Julia triangle's exact proportions.
- **risk**: at 32px the baseline can read as a plain zigzag if not for the color fills.

### c1trace-02 — "Poincaré Triad"
- **concept**: A tilted Poincaré (NN(n) vs NN(n+1)) ellipse cloud along the 45°
  identity line, where the three densest scatter clusters are rendered as the
  three large Julia-colored dots, with faint small same-hue points as the
  surrounding scatter members.
- **motif**: Poincaré / three-dot
- **colors**: all three, equal weight, arranged along the diagonal
- **register**: scientific, minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: honors the real SD1/SD2 ellipse geometry (elongated, diagonal)
  while making the dominant visual read exactly the Julia triad.
- **risk**: faint scatter dots vanish at 32px, leaving "3 dots in an ellipse" —
  still valid, but the Poincaré nuance is legible mainly at full size.

### c1trace-03 — "Tachogram Triad"
- **concept**: Three IBI bars of different heights (a tachogram / NN-interval
  sequence) rise from a shared baseline, each capped by one Julia dot; a thin
  trend line links the bar tops like a beat-to-beat plot.
- **motif**: IBI-gap / tachogram / three-dot
- **colors**: red, purple, green — purple as the tallest (peak) interval
- **register**: scientific, minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: bar-chart language reads instantly as "data/time-series",
  grounding the abstract triad in something a scientist recognizes as HRV output.
- **risk**: could be mistaken for a generic bar-chart icon if colors were muted.

### c1trace-04 — "Limit-Cycle Knot"
- **concept**: An asymmetric, organic closed loop (a Van der Pol relaxation-
  oscillation limit cycle, not a circle) with the three Julia dots riding the
  trajectory at uneven points, as if caught mid-orbit.
- **motif**: attractor / limit cycle / three-dot
- **colors**: purple (top lobe), red and green (side lobes)
- **register**: scientific, illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the lopsided, non-circular contour reads distinctly as a
  dynamical-systems trajectory rather than a generic ring, while still resolving
  into the familiar triad.
- **risk**: without dynamics-literate context, some viewers may just see "a blob".

### c1trace-05 — "Beat Graph"
- **concept**: Three dots wired into a triangle by connecting lines, each edge
  carrying a small ECG-style blip mid-span — three data points joined by a
  beat/rhythm line, per the lane's network reading. Includes a small "HRL"
  wordmark below (this studio's one text variant).
- **motif**: IBI-gap / ECG / three-dot / network
- **colors**: purple, green, red at the triangle's vertices
- **register**: scientific, typographic (light)
- **wordmark**: includes text ("HRL")
- **why_it_works**: literally shows "three data points connected by a beat line",
  directly satisfying the lane's suggested motif, with a compact wordmark for
  contexts needing a labeled mark.
- **risk**: the zigzag edges are the busiest element in the set — the text
  vanishes at 32px (expected/acceptable for a favicon-scale use, per brief).

### c1trace-06 — "Attractor Butterfly"
- **concept**: A Lorenz-style lemniscate (figure-eight/butterfly) with the two
  wing centers and the crossing node rendered as the three Julia dots — the
  attractor's own geometry supplies the triad.
- **motif**: attractor / Lorenz / three-dot
- **colors**: red and green at the wing centers, purple at the crossing
- **register**: minimalist, scientific
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: the infinity/butterfly shape is instantly recognizable as a
  nonlinear-dynamics attractor and is structurally different from every other
  "loop" mark in the set.
- **risk**: could read as a generic infinity symbol if the dot placement isn't
  noticed first.

### c1trace-07 — "Pulse Ring"
- **concept**: A thin heart-monitor ring (ECG wrapped into a circle) with three
  small radial pulse-blips 120° apart, each terminating in a Julia dot — a
  "beat propagating around a ring" pulse read.
- **motif**: pulse / ECG / three-dot
- **colors**: purple top, red and green at the lower two ring positions
- **register**: scientific, minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: perfect rotational symmetry and a literal monitor-ring frame
  make the "pulse" reading unmistakable while keeping the Julia triangle intact.
- **risk**: the small radial ticks can fuse into the dots at 32px, reducing to
  "ring + 3 dots" — still legible, just loses the pulse-blip detail.

### c1trace-08 — "IBI Bracket Triad"
- **concept**: Three beat spikes on a baseline, each capped by a Julia dot, with
  two measuring brackets above spanning beat-to-beat gaps — a direct, literal
  depiction of what NN/IBI intervals measure.
- **motif**: IBI-gap / ECG / three-dot
- **colors**: red, purple, green spikes; purple/green brackets for the two gaps
- **register**: scientific, minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: most scientifically explicit mark in the set — a domain
  expert sees "interval measurement" immediately, not just "three dots".
- **risk**: the bracket lines are the thinnest strokes in the set and are the
  first detail to disappear at 32px (spikes + dots remain legible).

### c1trace-09 — "Heart from a Heartbeat"
- **concept**: An abstract heart silhouette built entirely from a single ECG-
  style bent line (no cliché valentine curves); its two upper lobes and central
  dip are marked by the three Julia dots.
- **motif**: heart glyph / ECG / three-dot
- **colors**: red and green at the lobes, purple in the dip/center
- **register**: illustrative, minimalist
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: satisfies the brief's "heart glyph built from curves rather
  than a cliché valentine" directly, and is the cleanest, most universally
  legible mark in the set.
- **risk**: closest in spirit to a generic heart-outline icon if the dots were
  ever removed or miscolored.

### c1trace-10 — "Spiral Attractor"
- **concept**: A true logarithmic phase-space spiral (a damped oscillator /
  attractor trajectory converging on a fixed point), with three passes of the
  spiral marked by the three Julia dots at decreasing radius, ending near the
  center.
- **motif**: attractor / spiral / three-dot
- **colors**: red (outermost pass), green (mid), purple (innermost, converged)
- **register**: scientific, illustrative
- **wordmark**: icon-only
- **why_it_works**: directly matches the brief's explicit "spirals" vocabulary,
  and the converging composition reads as "settling into rhythm" — a fitting
  metaphor for HRV stabilization.
- **risk**: the multi-turn curve has the most path complexity in the set; must
  stay thick-stroked to avoid muddiness at small size (verified at 32px).

---

## Strongest picks
1. **c1trace-09** ("Heart from a Heartbeat") — cleanest silhouette, universally
   legible, most directly satisfies the brief's heart-glyph guidance.
2. **c1trace-01** ("Triad Pulse") — most efficient fusion: one line is both a
   correct QRS shape and the exact Julia triangle.
3. **c1trace-03** ("Tachogram Triad") / **c1trace-06** ("Attractor Butterfly") —
   tie: both ground the triad in an instantly-recognizable HRV/dynamics motif
   distinct from ECG, broadening the set's vocabulary.
